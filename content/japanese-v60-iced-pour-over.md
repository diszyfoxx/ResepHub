Japanese V60, also known as flash brew, brews hot coffee directly onto ice. Only 60% of the water is hot — the rest is ice in the server — so the aromatics are captured cold instead of lost to the air. The result is a clean, fresh, and aromatic iced coffee with more body than cold brew.

- ⏲️ Brew time: 2 min 30 sec
- 🍽️ Servings: 1 glass

## Pour Over Kit
- V60 Plastic Dripper
- V60 Server
- V60 Paper filter
- Kettle
- Digital scale
- Coffee Grinder

## Ingredients

- 20g coffee beans, medium-fine (a little finer than hot V60, like fine sand)
- 180g hot water at 90°C
- 120g ice in the server (large cubes melt slower, so less dilution)

## Directions

1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water, discard the water, then put the ice in the server.
2. Add 20g of coffee grounds into the V60. Wiggle to level the bed. Tare the scale.
3. **0:00** — Bloom: pour 60g of water, swirl gently so all grounds are wet, wait 45 seconds.
4. **0:45** — Pour in a slow spiral until the scale reads 120g.
5. **1:20** — Pour again until the scale reads 180g. Keep the stream low and thin — this matters more with a stove kettle than a gooseneck.
6. Drawdown should finish at **2:00–2:30**. If it runs longer, grind coarser next time; if it finishes too fast, grind finer.
7. Swirl the server, pour into a glass with **fresh ice**, and drink immediately. Don't let it sit.

## 15g Version

Same method, scaled down: 15g coffee, 135g hot water, 90g ice. Pour in pulses of 45g / 90g / 135g. Grind at 48 clicks (dial 4.8) on the Chestnut S5 Pro, versus 50 clicks (dial 5.0) for the 20g dose.

## Notes Per Bean

- **Gayo Wine (light-medium)** — 91–92°C, base recipe as-is. The winey sweetness comes out best here.
- **Karo Jasmine Infused** — 88°C, and grind slightly coarser than the base recipe. Infused beans extract aggressively; high temperature turns the jasmine aroma perfumey and bitter. This is the most sensitive of the three.
- **Cranberry Grapes Natural Anaerob** — 90°C. For a more intense fruit character, use a 1:14 ratio instead: 170g hot water + 110g ice. Don't go hotter — heat turns the funky notes into over-fermented flavors.

## Brew Log

First attempt on the Cranberry Grapes at 65 clicks and 94°C came out hollow in the middle, with muddled notes and muted aroma. The fix: **50 clicks and 90°C**, with the 60g / 120g / 180g pour pulses — the fruit notes opened up and the body filled in.

## Troubleshooting

- Sour or hollow → grind finer, or raise the temperature 1–2°C
- Bitter or astringent → grind coarser, or lower the temperature
- Thin like tea → the ice was too warm or too much, or raise the dose
