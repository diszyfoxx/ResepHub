The French Press method, also known as plunger pot, is an immersion brewing technique that produces a rich, full-bodied cup of coffee with natural oils and sediments for a bold flavor profile.

- ⏲️ Brew time: 4 minutes
- 🍽️ Servings: 2-4 cups

## French Press Kit
- French Press (1L capacity)
- Drip Kettle
- Digital scale
- Coffee grinder

## Ingredients Ratio
To make french press method you only need Coffe Beans (Coarse Grind) and Hot Water. For the ratio, i'd like to use **1:15 ratio**.

  Serving | Coffee Amount | Water Amount 
----------|---------------|---------------------
    1 Cup | 15g           | 225ml
    2 Cup | 30g           | 450ml
    4 CUp | 50g           | 750ml

## Directions

#### Preparation
- Grind Size: Ensure your coffee is ground Coarse, similar to the consistency of sea salt. If the grind is too fine, the coffee will taste overly bitter and the plunger will be difficult to press.
- Water Temperature: Avoid using boiling water (100°C). Let it sit for about 1 minute after boiling so the temperature drops to approximately 92–96°C.

#### Brewing
1. Preheat: Rinse the French Press carafe with hot water to stabilize the temperature, then discard the water.
2. The Bloom: Add the coffee grounds. Pour in just enough hot water to saturate them (about twice the weight of the coffee) and let it sit for 30 seconds. This releases $CO_2$ gas, allowing the flavors to develop fully.
3. Pour & Stir: Pour in the remaining water according to your ratio. Give it one gentle stir to ensure all grounds are fully submerged.
4. Steep: Place the lid on top (do not plunge yet). Let the coffee brew for 4 minutes.
5. The Press: Press the plunger down slowly and with constant pressure until it reaches the bottom. If you feel extreme resistance, your grind is likely too fine.
6. Serve: Pour the coffee into your mug immediately. Do not leave leftover coffee in the French Press, as it will continue to extract and become unpleasantly bitter.
